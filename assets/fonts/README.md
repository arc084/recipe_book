# Bundled fonts

All three ship with the app rather than being fetched at runtime, because the
app is offline by design. All three are licensed under the **SIL Open Font
License 1.1**, whose terms require that the copyright notice and licence travel
with the font files — which is what the `OFL-*.txt` files beside them are for.
Do not remove them.

| Font | Used for | Copyright | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inter | Nocturne (dark) headings and body | Copyright 2020 The Inter Project Authors — https://github.com/rsms/inter | [OFL-Inter.txt](OFL-Inter.txt) |
| Figtree | Organic (light) body | Copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors — https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree | [OFL-Figtree.txt](OFL-Figtree.txt) |
| Baloo 2 | Organic (light) headings | Copyright 2019 The Baloo 2 Project Authors — https://github.com/EkType/Baloo2 | [OFL-Baloo2.txt](OFL-Baloo2.txt) |

The OFL permits bundling and redistribution, including in a closed-source or
commercial application, provided the fonts are not sold on their own and the
notice above is kept. It does **not** impose any condition on the licence of the
software they are bundled with.
